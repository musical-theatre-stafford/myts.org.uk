Abbie was with MYTS as **Abbie Venables**, appearing in productions including
*Footloose*. She went on to co-create, produce and star in *Spraywatch: A
Beautiful Rescue* — a big-hearted musical comedy spoofing 1990s beach dramas,
from Manchester's Low Fat Productions. The show played the Edinburgh Festival
Fringe in 2026 to warm reviews, including five stars, with Abbie taking the
role of Rose.
