Laura grew up in Stoke-on-Trent and trained at Mountview Academy of Theatre
Arts. She went on to play Anne Boleyn in the 2023–24 UK & International Tour of
*Six the Musical*, and her stage credits also include *42 Balloons* at the
Vaudeville Theatre and *Ordinary Days* at the Cockpit Theatre, alongside panto
seasons at the Royal & Derngate.
