Tom played leads for MYTS including Ren in *Footloose*, Enjolras in
*Les Misérables* and Bernardo in *West Side Story*. He's gone on to work as a
musical director and orchestrator, with credits including the UK tours of
*Mean Girls* and *Sister Act*, *Muriel's Wedding* at the Curve, and associate
musical director on *Heathers* in the West End.
