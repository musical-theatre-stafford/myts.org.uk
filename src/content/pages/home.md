## Musical theatre for Stafford's young performers

MYTS is the junior division of [Musical Theatre Stafford](https://musicaltheatrestafford.org.uk).
We train young people aged 8–18 in singing, acting and dance, and stage a full musical at
Stafford Gatehouse Theatre each year.

Whether you're a prospective performer, a parent, or looking to book tickets to a show,
we'd love to hear from you.
